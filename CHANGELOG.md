# Changelog

## 1.6.0 (unreleased)

A pre-launch review of the whole mod. Everything here is a fix or a clearer word; nothing new to learn.

**Numbers that were wrong or confusing**
- **One "today".** Wherever you read "today" (Home, This session, Usage, the line above the prompt, reports) it is the same figure: every project's records, never below this chat's own share. The daily budget keeps counting each chat's own ledger, and where it is shown it says "counted toward the daily budget". A chat carried over from earlier days no longer shows its whole cost as today. When this chat's spend is not in the records yet it is counted in the project it runs in (a worktree's repository, or a row of its own), as one chat and one project, so the calendar, the day bars, the project rows and the shares by model and project all still add up to the week.
- **Percentages tell the truth.** Never "100%" before full, never "0%" once some is used, "under 1%" for a sliver, and a limit at or over 100% reads "used up until the reset" instead of "runs out early". Shares of the weekly limit count only the work since the window opened, and each one says what it is a share of.
- **One "today" on the line above the prompt too.** It is floored at this chat's own spend, the same as the pane, and so is the "Today is 3× your usual day" note. Context at 99.6% is 99% in every place, and alerts judge the figure the pane shows.
- **Calculations.** Two weekly limits stay two cards with their own names; "Give skills more room" is offered only when it adds room; the past seven days are counted on the calendar around clock changes; workflow stages are called routine or judgment by whole words; Haiku 5.5 long prompts and Claude 3 models are priced on the right card; money under a cent reads "<$0.01"; a terminal bar over its maximum can no longer throw.
- **Savings add up.** The savings card is one story in your unit (the difference of the two figures drawn), and the weekly chart, the daily bars and the turn chart name their dashed lines.
- **Each saving idea says what its share is of.** On a plan every figure in an idea's sentence is a share of the weekly limit and says so ("would have saved about 35%–43% of your weekly limit"), one figure to a sentence, never a share of another figure; the re-read cost after a model switch is in the same unit, and dollars stay for an API key. More than a whole limit is said in weeks once ("2.9 weeks’ worth"), never "weeks’ worth of your weekly limit". The status tool gives the past seven days as a share beside the dollars, as it does this chat and today. The two share bars on Usage are named with the shares in their legends.

**Models, budgets and workflows**
- **A model you choose wins,** from the moment you switch (with /model, the picker, the desktop app or an IDE) and stays chosen when a budget is reached later. "Always" no longer keeps overriding it.
- **Agents at once** really caps a fan-out, an agent that starts before its workflow's reply is back counts, and a stopped workflow resumed later starts its agents at once. Stop on a workflow whose stop fails no longer marks it as stopping.
- **Hold prompts** holds only your own prompts; background notifications and scheduled runs still arrive, and held words go after a draft, never over it. Where there is no prompt box to give them back to (a headless run, the phone), the message repeats your words instead of saying they are back.
- **After /clear or a resume,** what the earlier conversation cost today stays in the daily budget. Cost, budget, limit and context alerts are said once however many hooks ask together.
- **At "Cheaper model"** every request switches as the alert says, helpers and workflow agents included; a model chosen in the pane or by you stays.
- **Start fresh** fills the prompt box only when it is empty and never writes over a draft.
- **Prices:** Sonnet 5.5 cache reads are priced at $0.10 per million tokens, so its costs, shares and the Opus-to-Sonnet savings are right. Cost figures say they are at standard speed and global routing.
- **"Run every agent on Sonnet"** is the name of that answer to the go-ahead question, and it says it includes stages the script put on a stronger model. If Ask me first cannot tell whether anyone is there, it asks anyway.
- **Helper models fail softly.** An empty reply, a refusal or a provider error from the helper model hands the job to the cheaper model, a refusal on a request sent to the cheaper model goes again on the usual one, and a reply that used its whole cap says it was cut off.
- **Switching model or lowering effort says what it costs once:** the next request re-reads the chat, and the toast, the model advice and the *Think less past the warning* help say about how much. The cheaper-model settings say that the whole chat moves at a reached budget, and suggest Sonnet for a smaller step down.
- **Claude is told the budget plainly.** With a budget set to Warn it is not told to stop; otherwise it is told which budget, that the figure is an estimate and that a new request still stands. The long-run skill asks the status tool at each checkpoint, and a prompt carries a limits line whenever a limit is past its alert or on course to run out early.
- **The status tool loads only when Claude looks for it,** instead of riding in every request.
- **A paused workflow stays paused.** A held agent sleeps five seconds between looks and writes its state only when the reason changes, an interrupted turn stops waiting, and a reload no longer releases the agents of a run that is still paused. *Restore defaults* saves the settings one at a time from a written-down list and carries on after each reload; the confirmation says the dashboard may blink.
- **The handoff note** is skipped when the chat is nearly full and, for a compaction you did not ask for, when it has been idle, since it is one more request over the whole conversation; a note that takes over 20 seconds no longer holds the compaction up. The prompt tells Claude to leave secrets out, a reply that is not a note (too short, or no Goal) is not saved, a `.gitignore` in the folder that does not ignore everything is rewritten, and *Start fresh* asks Claude to read the note as notes, say the goal and next steps, and wait for your go-ahead.

**What waits for you**
- **One count.** Chats, pull requests and a workflow asking for its go-ahead are counted once and said in the same words on Home, the line above the prompt and the phone, with the go-ahead drawn first and "Go to the chat to answer" as its one button. A permission waiting in a running chat says where to switch to answer it.
- **Honest when it can't tell.** If Claude Code's records can't be read Home says so and offers "Read projects again". Being offline no longer reads as "gh isn't signed in", and a pull request titled "@@SIGNEDOUT" can't blank the list.
- **Pull requests** are asked for once in five minutes for the whole computer, not once per chat.
- **The suggestion to make a workflow** appears for prompts you type, send from the desktop app or from your phone.
- **Reply tries the `SendMessage` tool first** and falls back to the desktop app's older `send_message`, and says plainly when neither works. Replies, archiving and the plan card use the app's own session tools, which are not part of the documented mod interface.
- **The list of running chats is read once a minute for the whole computer** and shared, other chats' workflow folders are listed only for chats that had workflow agents or were busy in the last quarter hour, and finished runs beyond the newest five leave the chat's state. When ten or more chats were read and none held a usage line, a folder or a topic, Home says "Can't tell what's waiting" instead of giving an all clear.

**Safer**
- **Git runs without optional locks and with `core.fsmonitor` off** in the folders of recent chats, so a repository you never trusted can't make git run a program or rewrite its index.
- **Text from your records is treated as data** everywhere it travels: the handoff note, skill files, drafts, the prompts sent to the helper model, and the status tool's answer can't close their own frames, and folder, branch and entry names are cleaned of control and direction-changing characters. A model or project named `toString` or `valueOf` can no longer corrupt a tally.
- **A skill that is a symbolic link** is not read or rewritten by a fix, and the handoff note is not written through a link. A settings file, CLAUDE.md or skill that can't be read is never taken for an empty one.
- **Settings, skills, CLAUDE.md and saved reports follow `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`,** the pane names the folder it really saves to, and out-of-range settings are held to the bounds the pane enforces.
- **The self-check says "Matches its own manifest",** finds an installed copy under any config folder, keeps its verdict per folder and manifest, and names files added where Claude Code would load them.
- **The phone address is marked sensitive,** its description and the pane warn that a waiting chat's title and a short excerpt go there and that a public topic can be read by anyone who guesses it, and the examples use a long random topic.
- **A bad drawing can't blank the pane:** control characters and half a surrogate pair are removed before they reach a drawing.
- **Invisible text is removed** (tag characters, variation selectors, soft hyphens, fillers, line separators) from everything that comes from outside, and a link holding control or direction characters is refused.
- **A drafted skill is saved with only a name, a description and plain keys:** tool permissions, hooks, model, shell lines, hidden comments and inline commands are removed, the draft is shown as the exact file, and a check row says it holds none.
- **The status tool's overview gives counts.** Titles and excerpts of other chats come only with its *waiting* topic, which asks you first, and a deny rule of yours stands.
- **Turning *Read every project* off forgets what it kept,** and Settings has **Forget stored data…**, which asks first and clears everything the mod stored, spending ledgers included, and starts over what the mod held in memory, so nothing you forgot comes back at its next write. The option text says what is kept.
- **The file a pending Save change will write is held in the mod,** not in state the pane can read.
- **The notification address is never drawn again after saving:** only its host, and "(address hidden)".

**Looks and words**
- **One name for each thing:** chat for a session, budget for money you set, limit only for plan limits, Home for the first tab, Usage for spending, ToBeBestMe spelled one way.
- **Color means status.** Red words hold 4.5:1 on a dark and a white pane; the weekly Opus limit is a lighter blue than the weekly limit, no longer the pink that read as "nearly out"; no project, category or limit color is red, amber or green; each model keeps one color wherever it is drawn; a legend dot is the color of its bar.
- **Hero figures take their status colors:** amber past an alert, red when nearly out, green when well, the neutral accent for information, with a glow only while the figure is live. The report card's big figure wears the same pair for its status. The pink dots that pulsed over Today's bar are gone: motion now only means something is working.
- **Each tab leads with its answer.** This session's one filled button is the fix for its headline; Usage leads with the limits and the spending and the report card is one row of buttons; the report card wraps what it says instead of cutting it.
- **Narrow panes and phones:** labels stack over values below about 60 columns, the six tabs are two rows of three on a phone, the line above the prompt keeps "about 33 hours" whole, and the words beside Home's big figure no longer start above its edge.
- **One segmented control for every choice,** and each control has one home (the cheaper model and the usage alert in Settings, budgets and how costs show on Usage, the workflow check on Workflows).
- **Plain errors.** Settings never shows a developer error; it says what to do. A dimmed button always says why, and repeated buttons carry names that say whose they are ("Set aside: Release").
- **Forecasts hold still.** The week chart, runway bars and Usage forecast are drawn at the quarter hour, a drawing nothing changed in is handed to the app as the same markup for the whole visit to a tab, so the first seconds of a tab no longer swap every picture, and a running workflow's timeline is rebuilt at most once a minute. Polls redraw the pane only when what they read differs.
- **Loops carry on when figures change.** The pulse, halo, sheen and live ring start a tab-age into their cycle, so a drawing whose figures change no longer makes them start over, and a loop that has run its course is drawn finished.
- **No forecast or pace alert until 8% of the week (about 13 hours) has passed,** and the pane says "Too early in the week to forecast". A warning that no longer holds is forgotten, so a real one can still fire. Forecast spans are said roughly ("about 33 hours") and headlines say "on pace to run out".
- **Easier by keyboard and screen reader.** Every choice is named for its setting ("Send notifications: Off, selected"), the open tab says it is open, every field keeps a label, opening a reply, a confirmation, *Other…* or an address moves the keyboard to its field or the safe button, failures stay 15 seconds and are kept in Activity, and **Show details** is remembered across chats. The saved report card has a role, a label and a title, and hidden project names stay hidden.
- **No lone card beside a blank half.** When the main column has nothing (first run, records that can't be read, a go-ahead waiting) Home, Usage and Workflows use the full width. Open chats titles wrap instead of being cut, the run card's budget is labeled "Run budget" and says what it does, and with Show details on a heading's count stays beside it and its words start at its edge, inside the card they belong to.
- **Colors keep their meaning in a light pane too:** a hero figure's gradient keeps the hue of its status (amber stays orange, not brown), the week's bars are their project's own color, and no project wears the weekly Opus limit's blue.
- **Calmer motion.** The shimmer on projected use plays twice and the run-out marker pulses three times; only a live dot loops, and all of it is off with reduced motion set.
- **Words:** Notification Center, color and curly apostrophes inside words; "dashboard" for the interface and "agents" inside a workflow; Settings ends with "independent, not made by Anthropic" and the trademark line, and the plugin and marketplace descriptions say it too.

**Docs and release**
- **The README, the site and the launch copy lead with what ToBeBestMe is:** a mod that runs inside Claude Code itself, with no server, no sign-up and no API key.
- **SECURITY.md lists everything the mod reads, writes, runs and sends,** including replies sent to other chats, archiving, stopping a workflow, the files a fix can write, the notification text, the status tool's answer, and the whole settings file held in memory while a fix waits for Save change. It explains how to verify an install and a download, and that v1.5.0 has no provenance and is superseded.
- **Releases publish the zip's own SHA-256** beside `SHA256SUMS`, the workflow refuses a tag whose commit is not on `main` and keeps no credentials in the checkout. CONTRIBUTING.md describes the real release process.
- **The settings table, names and defaults in the README and DESIGN.md match the Settings tab,** and the manifest names a homepage, repository and license. TRADEMARKS.md names the project and its canonical repository.
- **Development:** the end-to-end suite no longer needs a sanitizer library; its drawing check applies the mod's own rule instead. Every pull request and push to `main` now runs the scripts' syntax check, the search-program test (`tests/scan.test.mjs`, which runs the shell and awk readers on a fixture transcript and holds them to the JavaScript reader) on Linux and macOS, `claude plugin validate --strict` and the test kit, and a release runs the same checks before it signs provenance. Dependabot watches the actions and the end-to-end suite, pull requests and issues have templates, `tsc -p .` checks the hooks and types, `.editorconfig` and `.prettierrc` describe the style, and CONTRIBUTING.md has the prerequisites and the exact commands. Test counts are no longer written down anywhere.
- **README, SECURITY.md and DESIGN.md match the code:** an Accessibility section, an independence line, the limits of the app's session tools, what the handoff note costs and when it is skipped, what the mod keeps and how to forget it, and the status tool's overview and waiting topic.
- **Claims match their sources.** The Skills text says Claude Code reports a full skill listing in `/doctor` and its debug log, not in the conversation; the weekly-limit text says what the mod forecasts and never what a plan has; two citations in DESIGN.md now point at pages that say what the sentence says.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-03)

- **Weekly limits only.** ToBeBestMe forecasts weekly limits, so Home, the line above the prompt, alerts, the pace warning, the model adviser, Usage, the status tool, the phone lobby and the report card are about the weekly limit and any model's own weekly limit. Any other window Claude Code reports is listed as a limit of its own, after the weekly ones and without a forecast. Settings and the description of *Alert at* say weekly.
- **The report card says something new under the figure.** Under "82% of my weekly limit" it now gives where the limit is headed (when it runs out, or the pace to the reset) instead of repeating the figure as "work worth 82% of the limit"; the models bar is one continuous rounded bar, with a small model's segment kept visible inside it.
- **Alerts that feel alive.** When this chat starts waiting on you (a permission, a question, or a finished answer waiting for your next message) one alert goes out for that wait, by default, even with Notifications off (*Alert when this chat waits for you*). It uses Claude Code's own notification channel, falling back to the computer's Notification Center only where that shows no words, and plays a chime through the engine's own `$.audio.play` (no more `afplay`/`paplay` unless the engine has no player). *Say alerts aloud* speaks a few words with `$.audio.speak`. The alert is rate-limited like every other (once per wait, four in ten minutes), says who is waiting and never the command, and a line under an open question says what else waits.
- **The next prompt, suggested.** Once context passes its warning (60% by default) the dim suggestion offers `/compact` with the plan kept, and after a limit warning `/tobebestme-handoff`, once per crossing or window; Tab takes it, and nothing is typed for you (*Suggest the next prompt*).
- **A status tool for Claude.** `mcp__tobebestme__status` (read-only, allowed without a prompt) answers where limits, context, cost, waiting chats and running workflows stand, so you can just ask Claude.
- **Pull requests and CI in Needs you.** With the GitHub CLI installed and signed in, your open pull requests that need you appear: review requested, changes requested, checks failing, merge conflicts, ready to merge; open or copy the link. Read in the background at most every five minutes with `gh`'s own read queries; with no `gh` or no sign-in Settings says why (*Pull requests and CI in Needs you*).
- **A weekly report card.** On Usage: the week as one image (figure, days by project, days used and streak, top projects, models, workflows and savings), saved as an SVG and, where a converter exists, a PNG, with a short text to paste. Project names can be hidden first.
- **The phone gets a lobby.** On the mobile app, what waits for you with the replies that exist, the tightest limit with its run-out, and running workflows.

- **The week calendar fills its row in the app.** The desktop app drew it at the browser's default 300px, squashing its labels and putting the bars under the wrong days. It now has its own size, and the day buttons share its width. The test renderer now sizes drawings the way the app does, so the check catches this.
- **Limit names read as phrases**: "of your weekly limit, all models", not "of your weekly · all models".
- **Every tab opens on its one answer.** This session leads with its cost and the context window against the point Claude Code compacts, with a ghost for the next turns and a bar per turn, and says whether it will make it. Usage leads with the forecast of the limit that matters most and where the week went (days by project, projects, models). Workflows leads with what is running as runways of cost against limit, or the month's cost and the part of it that could have been saved. Skills leads with the listing as a runway against its room, and Settings with how much is on autopilot and whether everything it reads could be read.
- **Pick up folds its rows.** The latest project is drawn whole and the others are a line each until opened, as Needs you does; Skill ideas shows three.
- **Light mode is tested.** Words and marks in drawings have light-theme colors (the white "Now", gray labels, amber and red), words drawn as text use colors that read on both panes, and the e2e suite checks the real states on a light pane for contrast.
- **The week's first day starts when the window did.** Its bar no longer counts that morning, from the hours the records now keep.
- **Privacy wording matches what it does.** Two footnotes said "Nothing leaves your computer". They now say reading Claude Code's records sends nothing anywhere, and SECURITY.md lists the lines *Tell Claude where the budget stands* adds to a prompt when a limit is close, and the handoff-note pointer after a compaction.
- **One number, one name.** The same limit now reads the same everywhere: forecast spans in alerts and headlines are rounded the way Home says them ("about 33 hours", not "a day"), models are "Opus 5.5" rather than `opus-5-5`, the cheaper model is capitalized ("Helpers now use Haiku"), and a pace is labeled as the projection at the reset. Today, in every session, is never below this session's own figure, and the band names them apart ("This session … of the weekly limit · Today, all sessions: …"). Free space on This session is everything not in use, with the part kept for compaction shown as part of it.
- **Weeks, not 300%.** A workflow cost beyond the whole weekly limit says "about 3 weeks’ worth", with "estimated at list prices"; savings lead with the share of the limit and give dollars second. The first saving has the one filled button and the rest are quiet text. The spending limit on a running workflow is "Spending limit, then hold new agents", so it no longer shares a name with the Pause new agents button, and a timeline of agents only a few seconds old is not drawn as two specks.
- **Narrow panes are designed.** Values stack under their labels, tables drop bars before numbers, Open sessions wrap their names and show the project (the branch and path are in the hover card), the permission label sits on its own line without repeating itself, and the line above the prompt keeps its headline and button on a phone.
- **Quieter secondary buttons, clearer empty states.** Copy resume command, Set aside, Copy run ID and Search again are quiet text; "Archive session…" is no longer dimmed; "Use Haiku for helpers" and "Switch this conversation to Sonnet" no longer compete with different advice. Empty Workflows is one column with a Start a workflow button, empty Skills says what a skill is and offers Build your first skill, empty Usage has no card of nothing, and an unreadable records folder is drawn as an error with a next step. Get more from Claude says what the count is.

- **The desktop pane gets a cinematic pass** (the terminal is unchanged). Big figures are gradients in each tab's colors (amber or red once a limit is past its alert), with a soft bloom of their own colors under the digits. ## 1.4.0

- **Your model choice wins.** A model you switch to with `/model` is no longer overridden by Always mode, a budget or a dashboard choice made earlier: the session stays on it and says so. Before, Always silently sent every request, the main conversation too, to the cheaper model, so a switch seemed to do nothing. While Always is sending this conversation to the cheaper model, the dashboard now says so, with a one-press **Turn Always off**.
- **Workflows end on time.** A run is now seen to finish with the pane closed, so the line above the prompt stops saying "running" and the "workflow finished" notification goes out.
- **Notifications go out once per computer.** Each alert has a stable key and a record every session shares, with at most four notifications in any ten minutes. They are sent in the background, so a sound never holds up the pane.
- **Suggested workflows.** A request that looks like a job for a workflow is suggested as one, with the kind, its stages and the model for each.
- **Run spending limits and the weekly-limit share.** A workflow can pause new agents past a spending limit. Costs are also shown as a share of your weekly limit, for plans that bill that way.
- **Ways to spend less** come from your own finished runs, as ranges, with a follow-up once you adopt one.
- **Narrow panes.** Tables drop the columns that don't fit, the usage forecast keeps 11px labels on a phone-width pane, and table cells keep a gutter.
- **Clearer actions.** Buttons say what they do ("Review workflow", "Refresh runs", "Copy run ID"), and the desktop tab bar is a segmented control, so the one primary button per view stands out.
- **Runways instead of rings.** The three concentric usage rings, which read as a watch face, are replaced by a runway per item: the fill is what is used, a strand under it is how much of the window has passed, and a limit that would run out early shows its run-out and the dry stretch to the reset. It is on the This session tiles, the Home limit tile and the line above the prompt, and in block characters on the terminal. A design panel of four directions was judged on distinctiveness, clarity and pace; this one won.
- **Animated chart tooltips** on the usage forecast. They fade in over 150 ms, and appear at once with reduced motion set.
- **Tamper evidence.** Releases ship SHA-256 checksums and build provenance. ToBeBestMe checks its own files against its release once a day.
- **Security.** Untrusted text is cleaned before display, keys from other sessions are checked, session ids are quoted, the handoff note is treated as data, and the store is capped by size.
- **A calmer, structured layout.** Sections are a heading above a faint grouped fill instead of bordered cards with icon tiles; the tabs sit in one segmented track; Home leads with the tightest limit as a full-width runway and one strip of figures; the most pressing waiting chat is drawn whole and the rest in one line each; on a wide pane Home, This session, Usage, Workflows, Context and Settings are two columns, with figures and controls beside the work. One primary button per view is enforced.
- **Fewer tabs.** Context is part of This session now (six tabs, and `/tobebestme context` still opens it). Each control lives in one place (budgets on Usage, the workflow check on Workflows). A section's explaining line shows under Show details, so the page stays quiet; the lines that carry a fact (how old a plan reading is, what notifications can show) stay. Usage draws a limit as the same runway as Home and the line above the prompt, with the history chart under Show details. The line above the prompt is two lines: the headline and its cost, then the figures.
- **Get more from Claude.** Home suggests up to three things at a time, each one press away, and puts one off for a month with Not now. They come from your own settings and records, never a fixed list: what the records show costing you (MCP servers re-sent with every request, skills that never run) comes first, then habits you could start (notifications, a skill for a prompt you repeat, a memory file, a first workflow, a spending guard). The card says how many habits are in place and goes away when there is nothing left to suggest.
- **A welcome tour.** The first time, four steps show the real thing and not a picture of it: the line above the prompt as it is now, what waits for you, your tightest limit laid out on its calendar, and the habits. It waits while a chat needs an answer, and `/tobebestme tour` brings it back. What it remembers (the tour is done, which suggestions were put off) is one small record in the shared store, `habits:v1`.
- **The week on a calendar.** The limit that matters most is drawn large on Home and Usage: the window's days (or hours) along the bottom, Now over the playhead, and where it runs out and when it resets in words over their posts. The smaller runways keep their short form.
- **Ask me first is a real gate.** It asks in the chat's own question dialog, which a hook can wait on for as long as you take, and a workflow agent held by a pause waits in short process naps. Before, both gave way after ten seconds and the run started anyway.
- **Notifications.** One store record per event, a claim at a time, `--` before the words of a system notification, an encoded phone header for non-ASCII titles, and budget notices that name their amount.
- **Costs as a share of your weekly limit.** On a Claude plan what runs out is the limit, not dollars, so by default every cost (Home, Usage, Workflows, the line above the prompt, the status line and the line under each answer) shows as a share of the weekly limit, estimated from this week's work at list prices against how far the limit has moved. Usage › Show costs as switches between Automatic, Share of limit and Dollars. Budgets stay in dollars.
